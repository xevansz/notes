# Spiritual Gifts — The Four Types of Tongues

> *"Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I do not want you to be ignorant."*
> — 1 Corinthians 12:1 [NKJV]

---

## The Four Types of Tongues in Scripture

### 1. Demonic / Counterfeit Influence
- Arises from demonic influence in spirit among those who do not genuinely seek God's gift, or through counterfeit practices taught by peer pressure.
- Rooted in carnal desires to appear unique rather than seeking edification from God.

---

### 2. Prophetical Tongues (For the Church — Requires Interpretation)

> *"I wish you all spoke with tongues, but even more that you prophesied; for he who prophesies is greater than he who speaks with tongues, unless indeed he interprets, that the church may receive edification."*
> — **1 Corinthians 14:5 [NKJV]**

- **Purpose**: Corporate edification.
- Paul explains that prophecy edifies the entire church, and when tongues are interpreted, they carry that same edifying power for the body of believers.

---

### 3. Personal Tongues as Known Human Languages (Sign to Unbelievers)

> *"And when this sound occurred, the multitude came together, and were confused, because everyone heard them speak in his own language."*
> — **Acts 2:6 [NKJV]**

- Here, the tongues spoken were recognizable human languages.
- They did not require an interpreter because native speakers from various nations understood them directly as a miraculous sign.

---

### 4. Personal Tongues for Private Prayer (Only God Understands)

> *"For he who speaks in a tongue does not speak to men but to God, for no one understands him; however, in the Spirit he speaks mysteries."*
> — **1 Corinthians 14:2 [NKJV]**

- **Distinction from Acts**: In Acts, men understood the language; here, Paul explicitly states *no man understands him* because he is speaking mysteries in the Spirit directly to God.
- Scripture does not contradict itself; rather, Paul is describing a distinct private devotional prayer tongue.

---

## 🕊️ Seeking with Sincerity & Faith

> *"If a son asks for bread from any father among you, will he give him a stone? Or if he asks for a fish, will he give him a serpent instead of a fish? If you then, being evil, know how to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him!"*
> — **Luke 11:11–13 / Matthew 7:9–11 [NKJV]**

- When you earnestly seek God for spiritual gifts, He will not give a counterfeit or demonic spirit. God protects His children when they ask in earnest faith.
- **1 Corinthians 12:31 [NKJV]**: *"But earnestly desire the best gifts. And yet I show you a more excellent way."*
- **1 Corinthians 14:38 [NKJV]**: *"But if anyone is ignorant, let him be ignorant."*

---

## 📌 Conclusion

Paul is not anti-tongues; he is establishing proper divine order. Prophecy has public priority because it edifies the whole gathering, whereas personal tongues edify the individual believer unless accompanied by an interpretation.
