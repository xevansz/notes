# Interview & Communication Tips

> *"Be a blank canvas. Don't be afraid to fill it. Make it bloom."*
> *"The most important tool is respect."*

---

## 🎤 The 3-Step Self-Introduction Formula

Avoid boring recitations of job titles. Structure introductions to hook, prove, and invite:

1. **The Hook**:
   - Don't start with credentials or job titles.
   - Start with **what you do** and **who you do it for**.
2. **The Proof & Unique Angle**:
   - Share why you are passionate about the domain; highlight your unique spin.
   - *Example*: *"I am a productivity enthusiast who believes that the right system and process can set creativity free."*
3. **The Invitation**:
   - Hand the conversation back with a thoughtful, open-ended question that prompts their engagement.

---

## ⚡ Closing Key Skill Gaps

1. **Figure Things Out Independently**: Don't wait for hand-holding; dive into source code, logs, and docs.
2. **Handle Professional Disagreements**: Debate ideas with data, humility, and principles rather than ego.
3. **Communication Principle**: Make messages **short, actionable, and opinionated**.
4. **Presentation & Presence**: Work on clarity of speech, pacing, and confident body language.
5. **Stop Underselling Yourself**: Never diminish your capabilities or downplay achievements to comfort others.

---

## 🧱 Proof of Work

- Don't just claim skills—show receipts.
- Maintain public artifacts: **GitHub repositories**, technical writeups, and **LinkedIn** case studies.
- Build tangible end-to-end projects.
